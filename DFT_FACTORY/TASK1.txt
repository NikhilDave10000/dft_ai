### TASK 1: The Definitive Master Dictionary of ATPG Variables

#### Phase 0: Netlist Ingestion & Model Primitives
*Before `run_build_model`. How TestMAX translates Verilog into mathematical logic.*
*   **`set_netlist -redefined_module <first | last>`:** If macro libraries overlap with the top-level netlist, this dictates which logic cone is kept. Silently adds or removes thousands of gates from ATPG's visibility.
*   **`set_build -empty_box vs. -black_box`:** Critical for RAMs/IPs. `-empty_box` drives unconnected outputs to `Z`. `-black_box` forces outputs to `X`, causing massive X-propagation and downstream coverage loss.
*   **`set_rules <rule_id> <warning | error>`:** Elevating rules (e.g., N5 for unknown clocks, B12 for bidirectionals) determines if the tool is allowed to build the model or aborts DRC entirely.

#### Phase 1: Fault List Manipulation (The Coverage Denominator)
*Between `run_build_model` and `run_atpg`. Altering the total fault count changes the final percentage, even if pattern count stays identical.*
*   **`set_faults -model <stuck | transition | path_delay | bridging | iddq>`:** The foundation. Determines the entire timing/physics requirement.
*   **`set_faults -pt_credit 0`:** Determines if "Possibly Detected" (PT) faults get partial credit (e.g., 50%) in the final coverage calculation.
*   **`remove_faults -instance <hierarchy>` / `add_nofaults`:** Explicitly deletes blocks from the fault list. *The classic CAD hack:* Shrinking the denominator mathematically inflates the final coverage percentage.
*   **`add_atpg_constraints -no_detection_credit <file>`:** Deeply advanced. Simulates good-machine logic accurately through a cell, but **refuses** to grant fault detection credit if the path passes through it. Forces DT faults to AU to stress-test alternative routing.

#### Phase 2: Structural Learning & Intelligence
*Before `run_build_model`. Pre-computes logic relationships to aid the solver later.*
*   **`set_learning -implication <low | medium | high>`:** `high` forces the model to learn indirect logic relationships (e.g., "If A=1, B must be 0"). Prevents ATPG dead-ends but spikes RAM and build time.
*   **`set_learning -common_input`:** Groups gates with common inputs to simplify the logic cone.
*   **`set_learning -equivalent_latches`:** Mathematically compresses the model by grouping latches with identical clock/data cones.
*   **`set_learning -max_feedback_sources <N>`:** Limits analysis of async feedback loops. Crucial for preventing `run_build_model` from hanging on complex async designs.

#### Phase 3: Simulation Integrity & X-State Management
*Before `run_drc`. Controls how unknown states are handled.*
*   **`set_simulation -strong_bidi_fill`:** Actively drives floating bidirectional ports to known states. Prevents floating I2C pins from propagating `X` states that mask out hundreds of good faults.
*   **`set_simulation -check_padding_patterns`:** Verifies filler cells don't create sneak electrical paths.
*   **`set_simulation -resolve_unknowns <assign | propagate>`:** `propagate` lets X's flow (safe, lowers coverage). `assign` forces X's to 0/1 (dangerous, masks real defects, artificially inflates coverage).

#### Phase 4: Constraints, Contention & Silicon Safety
*Before `run_drc` or `run_atpg`. Restricts the solver to prevent physical silicon damage.*
*   **`set_contention bus -atpg -capture -multiple_on -severity <abort>`:** Forces ATPG to avoid patterns causing bus contention. Will categorize valid faults as AU (lowering coverage) to guarantee the chip doesn't short out.
*   **`add_pi_constraints <0 | 1> <port>`:** Hard-locks a pin. If misapplied (e.g., locking `scan_en` during LOS), triggers S1 blockages.
*   **`add_po_masks <port | -all>`:** Blinds the ATE to outputs. Forces ATPG to route fault effects internally to scan flops.
*   **`add_cell_constraints <0 | 1 | X | OX> <instance>`:** Forces a scan cell to a state or blinds the tool to its output (`OX`). Forces upstream logic cones into AU.
*   **`add_capture_masks`:** Prevents specific flip-flops from capturing data during capture, stabilizing X-states at the cost of fault coverage.
*   **`remove_x_source`:** The single most important command for recovering flat-scan coverage. Uncontrolled X's from black-box IPs mask out thousands of faults. Removing sources forces ATPG to find alternative paths.

#### Phase 5: Delay, SDC & At-Speed Physics
*Before `run_drc`. Dictates the timing mechanics for Transition/Path Delay.*
*   **`set_delay -launch_cycle <system_clock | last_shift | extra_shift>`:** LOC vs LOS vs LOES architecture.
*   **`set_delay -nopi_changes` & `-nopo_measures`:** Prevents PI toggling/PO strobing at-speed.
*   **`set_delay -two_clock_transition_optimization`:** Auto-disables Fast-Sequential, using an optimized structural algorithm exclusively for LOC.
*   **`set_delay -diagnostic_propagation`:** Forces off-path inputs to be stable. Maximizes diagnosis resolution but crashes total coverage.
*   **`set_delay -slow_equivalence`:** "Cheats" by testing transitions at slow shift speeds. Artificially inflates coverage.
*   **`set_delay -nodisturb`:** Strictly limits side-input switching.
*   **`set_delay -slackdata_for_atpg` & `-max_tmgn <float>`:** Links with PrimeTime to *only* target faults with timing margins smaller than the float value (Slack-Based Testing).
*   **`read_sdc -set_false_path` / `-set_multicycle_path`:** If omitted, ATPG spends days targeting paths that electrically don't matter or cannot be captured in one cycle.

#### Phase 6: ATPG Engine Search, Compaction & Fill
*Before `run_atpg`. Dictates how hard the math solver works.*
*   **`set_atpg -abort_limit <N>`:** Max backtracks before dropping a fault to AU. Scales CPU exponentially.
*   **`set_atpg -capture_cycles <d>`:** Activates Fast-Sequential to pulse clocks `d` times.
*   **`set_atpg -merge <off | low | medium | high>`:** Effort to compress multiple faults into one pattern.
*   **`set_atpg -fill <random | 0 | 1 | X | adjacent>`:** `random` finds unmodeled defects but causes power droop. `adjacent` copies nearest care-bit to limit switching.
*   **`set_atpg -decision <random | norandom>`:** `random` introduces stochastic diversity to break deterministic deadlocks during compaction.
*   **`set_atpg -analyze_untestable_faults`:** Pre-ATPG analysis to immediately drop AU faults, saving massive CPU runtime.
*   **`set_atpg -resim_atpg_pattern <test_gen>`:** Forces a secondary ATPG pass to recover coverage lost during X-masking.
*   **`set_atpg -target_clock_faults`:** Unlocks Fast-Sequential to target faults directly through clock pins.
*   **`set_atpg -ndetects <n>`:** Forces the tool to find `n` unique patterns for the exact same fault. Boosts defect screening but inflates pattern count.
*   **`set_atpg -basic_min_detects_per_pattern <N>`:** Terminates pattern generation if a pattern fails to detect at least `N` faults.
*   **`run_atpg -optimize_patterns`:** Ignores manual limits; dynamically self-tunes for the absolute minimum pattern set (2-clock basic scan only).
*   **`run_atpg -rerun_for_timing_exceptions`::** Runs engine twice to add constraints for SDC false paths.
*   **`run_atpg -random`:** Blasts design with random patterns with all clocking variations before deterministic ATPG.

#### Phase 7: Power Limiting
*Before `run_atpg`. Prevents thermal runaway.*
*   **`set_atpg -power_budget <n>`:** Max percentage of scan flops allowed to toggle during capture.
*   **`set_atpg -power_effort <high>`:** Forces strict adherence to power budget at massive CPU cost.
*   **`set_atpg -shift_power_budget <n>`:** Max toggle allowed during slow shift phase.
*   **`set_atpg -shift_controller_peak <n>`:** Limits peak switching per shift power group (DFTMAX).

#### Phase 8: Server Infrastructure, Flow Control & Diagnostics
*Before/During `run_atpg`. Prevents server crashes and manages advanced flows.*
*   **`set_atpg -time <max_secs_per_fault>`:** The ultimate safety net to prevent multi-week hangs on a single fault.
*   **`set_atpg -patterns <max_patterns>`:** Hard stops the run if ATE memory limits are hit.
*   **`set_atpg -coverage <max_percent>`:** Stops the run immediately once a target is hit.
*   **`set_atpg -checkpoint {time file}`:** Saves progress so a killed job can be resumed.
*   **`set_atpg -num_threads <d>`:** Shared-memory multithreading on a single server.
*   **`set_atpg -num_processes <d>` / `run_atpg -distributed`::** Spawns independent instances across an LSF farm. Warning: Can cause slight pattern count overruns.
*   **`run_atpg -observe_file <file>`:** Reads a test point list to show potential coverage benefits *as if* test points were inserted.
*   **`run_atpg -only_chain_diagnosis` / `-only_chain_test`:** Restricts run purely to generating chain diagnostic patterns (e.g., `set_atpg -add_setreset_test`).
*   **`run_atpg -streaming_xtol_chain_test`::** Generates patterns for DFTMAX Ultra X-tolerant chain diagnostics.

#### Phase 9: Post-ATPG Structural Analysis
*After `run_atpg`. When the engine is maxed out.*
*   **`analyze_test_points -target fault_class`::** Analyzes remaining AU faults and outputs a list of exact RTL nets where physical test points must be inserted by DFT Compiler to mathematically unblock the logic.
