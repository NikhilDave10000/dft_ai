# DFT Factory: TR Experiment Dossier (Task 1 Compliant)

## EXP 2.0: Pre-ATPG Model & Fault Baseline
*Establishing the mathematical foundation before engine manipulation.*

### EXP 2.0.1: Logic Perception & Learning Baseline [Task 1 Phase 0 & Phase 2]
*   **Hypothesis:** Pre-computing indirect logic relationships reduces ATPG backtracks, justifying increased RAM usage during build.
*   **Action:** Execute `run_build_model` with `set_learning -implication high` and `set_learning -equivalent_latches`. Compare against a build using default/low settings.
*   **Sweep Variables:** `set_learning -implication <low | high>`
*   **Observation Metrics:** Build RAM (GB), Build Time (s), baseline ATPG CPU Time (s).
*   **Success Criteria:** High implication reduces downstream ATPG CPU time by >10%, justifying the RAM cost.

### EXP 2.0.2: The Coverage Denominator Hack [Task 1 Phase 1]
*   **Hypothesis:** Manipulating the fault list denominator artificially inflates the final coverage percentage without changing a single pattern.
*   **Action:** Run baseline ATPG. Then, re-run using `set_faults -pt_credit 0`. Finally, re-run using `remove_faults -instance <i2c_core>`.
*   **Sweep Variables:** `set_faults -pt_credit <default | 0>`, `remove_faults` vs. no removal.
*   **Observation Metrics:** `Total Faults` (denominator), `Test Coverage %`.
*   **Success Criteria:** Document exactly how much PT credit and block removal inflate the numbers. Establish a rule that sign-off numbers must reflect raw, un-hacked denominators.

### EXP 2.0.3: The Theoretical Coverage Ceiling [Task 1 Phase 5 & Phase 6]
*   **Hypothesis:** Basic-scan TR provides the absolute maximum coverage, ignoring physical at-speed constraints.
*   **Action:** `set_delay -launch_cycle system_clock` (no STIL timing update). `set_atpg -capture_cycles 0`. `run_atpg -auto_compression`.
*   **Sweep Variables:** None. Single iteration.
*   **Observation Metrics:** `Test Coverage %`, `AU (Untestable) %`, `Pattern Count`.
*   **Success Criteria:** Establishes the "100%" line. All subsequent LOC/LOS coverage drops are measured as a delta from this number.

## EXP 2.1: Launch-On-Shift (LOS) Matrix
*Context: Launch on last shift pulse. High risk of `scan_en` timing violations.*

### EXP 2.1.1: Physical Constraint & ATE Masking Sweep [Task 1 Phase 4]
*   **Hypothesis:** Constraining PIs/POs during the narrow LOS window causes DRC blockages or alters fault propagation.
*   **Action:** Sweep the 16-case matrix.
    *   `PI0` (`set_delay -nopi_changes`) vs `PI1` (`set_delay -pi_changes`)
    *   `PO0` (`set_delay -nopo_measures`) vs `PO1` (`set_delay -po_measures`)
    *   `M1` (`add_po_masks -all`)
    *   `C1` (`add_pi_constraints 0 scan_en`)
*   **Sweep Variables:** The 16 combinations of PI/PO/M/C.
*   **Observation Metrics:** DRC Pass/Fail, `S1 Violation Count`, `M237 Warning Count`, `Test Coverage %`.
*   **Success Criteria:** Identify the "Golden LOS Setup" (Expected: `PI0_PO0_M1_C0`). Prove `C1` fails DRC due to S1 blockages.

### EXP 2.1.2: LOS Engine Determinism & Compaction [Task 1 Phase 6]
*   **Hypothesis:** Strict LOS physics cause deterministic deadlocks that can be broken by randomization or compaction tuning.
*   **Action:** Using Golden LOS Setup. Sweep randomization and merge effort.
*   **Sweep Variables:** `run_atpg -random` (on/off), `set_atpg -merge <off | high>`, `set_atpg -decision <random | norandom>`.
*   **Observation Metrics:** `Pattern Count` delta, `CPU Time` delta.
*   **Success Criteria:** Identify the combination that yields the lowest pattern count without increasing CPU time exponentially.

### EXP 2.1.3: LOS Server Safety & Abort Limits [Task 1 Phase 8]
*   **Hypothesis:** LOS hits an "abort brick wall" faster than LOC due to scan_en physics.
*   **Action:** Sweep the backtracking limit and strict CPU limits.
*   **Sweep Variables:** `set_atpg -abort_limit <10 | 50 | 500>`, `set_atpg -time <60>`.
*   **Observation Metrics:** `CPU Hours`, `Coverage Delta`.
*   **Success Criteria:** Define the exact abort limit where the CPU/Coverage curve flattens, proving higher limits are a waste of server farm resources.

## EXP 2.2: Launch-On-Capture (LOC) Matrix
*Context: Two fast functional pulses. Cleanest timing.*

### EXP 2.2.1: STIL "Timing Squeeze" Validation [Task 1 Phase 5 / Infrastructure]
*   **Hypothesis:** Injecting a 20ns at-speed window into a 100ns STIL frame closes timing without ATE margin violations.
*   **Action:** Apply `update_clock -wft _allclock_launch_WFT_ -pulse {85 95}` and `_allclock_capture_WFT_ -pulse {5 15}`. Execute `run_drc` ONLY.
*   **Sweep Variables:** Timing offsets (if primary 85/5 fails).
*   **Observation Metrics:** `P7 Violations` (Capture too early), `P8 Violations` (Capture too late).
*   **Success Criteria:** Zero P7/P8 violations. ATPG is strictly prohibited from running until this passes.

### EXP 2.2.2: Fast-Sequential Depth Resolution [Task 1 Phase 6]
*   **Hypothesis:** Un-scannable I2C state machine logic requires multi-cycle capture to propagate fault effects.
*   **Action:** 
    1. Run baseline LOC. Execute `report_summaries sequential_depths`.
    2. Python parses log for `Max Detect Depth` (assume `3`).
    3. Sweep capture cycles.
*   **Sweep Variables:** `set_atpg -capture_cycles <2 | 3 | 4>`.
*   **Observation Metrics:** `Test Coverage %`, `Fast-Sequential Pattern Count`.
*   **Success Criteria:** Coverage peaks exactly at the extracted depth. Higher cycles yield zero gain.

### EXP 2.2.3: LOC Contention Deep-Dive [Task 1 Phase 4 & Phase 6]
*   **Hypothesis:** Fast LOC pulses cause simultaneous switching noise that forces bus contention on the Wishbone interface.
*   **Action:** Compare baseline LOC vs. enabling contention prevention.
*   **Sweep Variables:** `set_atpg -post_capture_contention_prevention` (on/off).
*   **Observation Metrics:** `Test Coverage %` drop, `Contention Warning Count`.
*   **Success Criteria:** Quantify the exact coverage penalty required to guarantee the bus doesn't short out.
