COMMAND: add_display_gates

TITLE:
add_display_gates

DESCRIPTION:
Use this command to selectively add gates to the GSV.

SYNTAX:
add_display_gates [< pin_pathname | gate_id | -all>] [-color <blue | green | orange | pink | purple | yellow | white | default>] [-path { begin_gate_id end_gate_id }]

OPTIONS:
< pin_pathname | gate_id |
          -all> : Adds gates associated with the pin_pathname, gate ID, or all gates to the
            GSV. Choices pin_pathname , gate_id , and -all are
            only accepted in primitive view.
-color <blue | green | orange | pink | purple | yellow | white | default> : Designates a particular color for displaying selected gates in the graphical schematic
            viewer (GSV).
-path
            { begin_gate_id end_gate_id } : This option traces the path between two sequential elements in the GSV. You must
            specify two end-points that are not separated by any other sequential elements. A path,
            in this case, is considered to be the combinational logic between sequential
            elements.

EXAMPLES:
TEST> add_display_gates mux3/MUX2_UDP_1/Q
