COMMAND: remove_waveform_signals

TITLE:
remove_waveform_signals

DESCRIPTION:
Removes gates associated with the pin pathname, gate ID, net names, or all gates from the GSV.

SYNTAX:
remove_waveform_signals < pin_pathname | gate_id | net_names | -all>

OPTIONS:
pin_pathname | gate_id | net_names | -all : Specifies the pin pathname or the gate ID of the element whose simulation behavior is
            being modified. If you specify the -all option, TestMAX ATPG removes
            all gates from the GSV.
