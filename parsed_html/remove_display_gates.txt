COMMAND: remove_display_gates

TITLE:
remove_display_gates

DESCRIPTION:
Use this command to selectively remove gates displayed in the GSV.

SYNTAX:
remove_display_gates < pin_pathname | gate_id | -all>

OPTIONS:
pin_pathname | gate_id |
          -all : Removes gates associated with the pin_pathname, the gate ID, or all gates from the
            GSV.

EXAMPLES:
TEST> remove_display_gates mux3/MUX2_UDP_1/Q
