COMMAND: add_waveform_signals

TITLE:
add_waveform_signals

DESCRIPTION:
Adds waveforms associated with the pin_pathname , gate ID , or net_name , or all gates to the simulation waveform viewer.

SYNTAX:
add_waveform_signals < pin_pathname | gate_id | net_names | -all>

OPTIONS:
pin_pathname | gate_id | net_names | -all : Specifies the pin pathname or the gate ID of the element whose simulation behavior is
            being monitored. If you specify the -all option, TestMAX ATPG adds all
            gates to the simulation waveform viewer.
